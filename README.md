### What it does

Design inductive and resonant wireless charging links end to end: coil geometry,
mutual inductance and coupling, 3D fields, induced voltage, efficiency, rectifier
design, thermal and ferrite effects, foreign-object heating, and ICNIRP exposure,
with exports to Excel, Word, PDF, Touchstone and SPICE.

Free calculators for quick checks: [airinductsim.com/tools](https://airinductsim.com/tools)
